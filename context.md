# Project Context: Drug Inventory & Supply Chain Tracking System (DISCTS)

## 1. Background
In public health systems, distributing medical supplies efficiently from state warehouses down to rural medical institutions is a massive logistical challenge. Currently, a significant portion of this workflow relies on manual registers, disconnected spreadsheets, and delayed reporting. This fragmentation leads to severe operational blindspots.

## 2. The Core Problem
The lack of real-time visibility across the supply chain creates two major crises:
1. **At the Hospital Level:** Frequent stockouts of life-saving drugs, or conversely, hoarding of drugs that eventually expire and are wasted.
2. **At the Administration Level:** Inability to track vendor performance, shipment delays, or statewide consumption patterns in real-time.

The overarching mandate is to fulfill the "Seven Rights" of supply chain logistics: **Right Product, Right Quantity, Right Place, Right Time, Right Condition, Right Cost, and Right People.**

## 3. Target Audience & Personas
* **Super Admin / State Health Officer:** Needs a bird's-eye view of statewide stock levels, alerts for expiring drugs, and vendor SLA (Service Level Agreement) performance scores.
* **Warehouse Manager:** Needs to process supply orders, manage central inventory, and dispatch shipments to hospitals.
* **Hospital / Institution Pharmacist:** Needs a fast, low-friction way to receive shipments (often in areas with poor internet connectivity) and log daily ward-level consumption.
* **Vendors / Suppliers:** Need to view approved orders, generate shipments, and track their delivery SLAs.

## 4. Business Value & Hackathon Impact
By digitizing and connecting these disparate nodes into a single source of truth, the system will:
* **Eliminate Wastage:** By tracking batch expiries and moving near-expiry stock before it's too late.
* **Ensure Availability:** Through automated low-stock alerts and predictive reordering.
* **Enforce Accountability:** Through an immutable audit trail and vendor performance tracking.

For the hackathon, the impact lies in demonstrating **real-time synchronization**—showing how a drug scanned at a remote hospital instantly updates the state-level dashboard.
